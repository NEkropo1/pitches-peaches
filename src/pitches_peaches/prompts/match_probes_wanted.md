# probes — questions to put to the reader

Nobody has asked this reader anything yet, so leave them the questions they
should answer before this card is worth trusting. These collect more from them;
they do not challenge them. Generate one for:

- every technology named in the posting that the CV does not cover — "the
  posting lists Kafka; have you worked with it?"
- anything relevant the CV mentions only in passing, where a paragraph of
  detail would sharpen the prep — "you list mutual TLS in a stack line; did you
  run it in production, and what broke?"

`why` says what the answer would change. Keep it under eight questions; pick
the ones that would actually move a score or a gap.
