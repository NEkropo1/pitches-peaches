# probes — already collected, emit none

The reader has **already been asked** what was missing. Any answers they gave
are in the background you were given, under `extra_notes`, as `Q:`/`A:` pairs —
they were free to skip questions, so there may be several, one, or none. Either
way they have had their turn: emit an empty `probes` list.

Two consequences, and the second one is the point of doing it this way:

- Do not ask for anything that is already answered there. Re-asking a question
  someone has just answered reads as not having listened.
- **Their answers carry exactly the same weight as the CV.** A technology named
  in the posting and missing from the CV is *not* a gap if they told you they
  have it — it is a fit point, and the line they typed is a quotable source for
  it, on the same footing as anything they wrote in the CV itself.

So read `extra_notes` before you score, not after. This card is the only one
they will see, and it is being written with everything they know already in
front of you.
